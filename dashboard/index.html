How to Use STALKER

Simple guide — no finance knowledge needed

🌅

Open this dashboard every morning. STALKER scans at 8:30 AM and shows the best stocks for today.

2
🟢

Only consider BUY signals. Yellow WATCH means wait. Red AVOID means skip.

3
⚡

The Live Prices tab shows real-time updates every 20 seconds. The P&L shows your profit/loss from the morning entry.

2 losing trades in one day? Stop. The system shows a warning. Protect your capital.
