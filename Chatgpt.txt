I went through your documentation in detail. The surprising thing is: the problem is probably not that you don't have enough filters. The problem is that you have too many lagging filters and almost no proof that the factors actually predict future returns.

If I were reviewing this as a quant/developer, these are the biggest issues I'd focus on.

1. Biggest Mistake: You're Ranking Stocks, Not Predicting Returns

Your system calculates an "Alpha Score" from 8 models and then assumes the highest score stocks will outperform.

But nowhere in the document do I see:

Backtesting
Factor validation
Win-rate measurement per factor
Feature importance analysis

For example:

You give:

Relative Strength = 20%
Institutional = 20%
Structure = 15%
Technical = 12%
Sector = 12%

But why?

Why not:

RS = 40%
Technical = 5%
Sector = 25%

The weights appear manually chosen rather than statistically derived.

A common quant mistake:

"This indicator sounds good, so I'll give it 20%."

Markets don't care whether something sounds logical.

2. News Analysis Is Extremely Weak

You are checking keywords like:

profit
growth
upgrade
acquisition

and bearish words like:

loss
downgrade
debt
crisis

This creates a huge problem.

Example:

"Company profit growth slows significantly."

Contains:

profit
growth

Your model may classify it as bullish.

But market may crash the stock.

Keyword sentiment is notoriously unreliable.

Many hedge funds abandoned keyword systems years ago because context matters far more than keywords.

3. Your Relative Strength Is Too Short-Term

You calculate:

RS = 20-day stock return - 20-day Nifty return.

20 days is very noisy.

What happens?

Stock rallies:

+15% in 20 days.

Your system gives it huge RS.

But often that stock is already extended and due for profit booking.

Professional momentum systems often evaluate:

3 month
6 month
12 month

and avoid only using very short lookbacks.

4. You Are Buying Near Resistance

This is a very important flaw.

Opportunity Score gives:

+35 if within 3% of resistance
+15 if near 52-week high

Think about what that means.

Your system often selects stocks that:

already ran up
already became obvious
are sitting near a selling zone

Result:

You buy.

Resistance rejects.

Stock falls.

You see losses.

This is exactly what many retail traders experience.

5. You Never Verify Whether Your Signals Work

The EOD report measures:

Open → Close return

Momentum

But what you actually need is:

For every factor:

Without this, you don't know which signals actually contribute.

Universe:

Nifty 50
Midcaps
Blue chips

Question:

Are you using today's list of stocks when backtesting old dates?

If yes:

Huge survivorship bias.

Because:

Bad companies disappear.

Good companies remain.

Historical performance becomes artificially inflated.

You're using:

RSI

The same signal may be receiving 4-5 separate rewards.

This creates overconfidence.

Without this:
