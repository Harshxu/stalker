1. First Understand: There Are 3 Types of Profitable Short-Term Trades

You should NOT use the same strategy for every stock.

A) Momentum Breakout (Best for Intraday)

Stock suddenly becomes active due to:

earnings
news
heavy buyers
sector rally
institutional activity

Goal:

buy breakout
sell same day

Example:
Stock breaks previous high with huge volume.

B) Pullback Continuation (Safest)

Stock already in uptrend.
You buy when price temporarily dips to support.

This is often safer than breakout chasing.

C) Reversal Trade (Advanced)

Stock was falling heavily and suddenly reverses.

High risk.
Avoid until experienced.

2. THE MOST IMPORTANT THING: MARKET STRUCTURE

This alone filters 70% bad trades.

Learn These 4 Structures
Uptrend

Higher highs + higher lows

HH → HL → HH → HL

Best for buying.

Downtrend

Lower highs + lower lows

Avoid buying.

Range / Sideways

Price trapped between support/resistance.

Good for scalping only.

Breakout Structure

Price exits a range with strong volume.

Most profitable intraday setup.

Best Practical Rule

ONLY BUY IF:

overall market trend bullish
sector bullish
stock bullish
volume increasing

This is called:

“Multi-Layer Confirmation”
